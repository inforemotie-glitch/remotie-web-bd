02 About Remotie

We are not an agency you hire.
We are the team you never had to build.

Remotie is a dedicated growth marketing agency from Bangladesh, built on five-plus years of active industry experience bridging sales strategy, digital growth and operational management. We work at the intersection of business development and revenue scaling — engineering execution roadmaps for local and international clients, then running them. The result is streamlined onboarding, structured execution and targeted market penetration, without you adding a single line to payroll.

Strategic Market Expansion & Client Integration

We define precise operational agreements — scoping, deliverables and compensation models for specialised roles such as email outreach specialists — so expansion is methodical, scalable and legally sound. New markets get entered on a plan, not on a hunch.

Data-Driven Paid Acquisition & Funnel Architecture

Meta, Google and TikTok budgets built on evidence rather than instinct. We rebuild the tracking first, structure campaigns around real profit signals, then shape the funnel underneath so the traffic you pay for has somewhere worth landing.

Entrepreneurial Leadership & E-Commerce Operations

We have run the operations side ourselves, so we speak in margins and fulfilment, not impressions. That operator instinct shapes every recommendation — what to scale, what to cut, and what your business can actually absorb this quarter.

Content Ecosystems & Industry Networking

Content that compounds instead of disappearing: calendars, pillars and repurposing pipelines feeding every channel at once — reinforced by the industry relationships that turn visibility into partnerships, referrals and inbound demand.

How a Remotie engagement actually runs

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    A free strategy call plus an audit of your accounts, tracking, creative and funnel. You leave that call with findings whether or not you work with us.

  2. 02

    Assemble

    We build the pod your stage needs and set the scorecard: the two or three metrics that define a winning quarter, agreed before anything launches.

  3. 03

    Launch

    Tracking rebuilt, campaigns structured, calendar loaded, automations wired. Kickoff typically lands within 72 hours of onboarding paperwork.

  4. 04

    Compound

    Weekly iteration, monthly reporting, quarterly strategy resets. Every learning is written into the system so results build instead of resetting.

Built in Bangladesh. Operating on your timezone.

Remotie is headquartered in Dhaka and works with founders and marketing leads across South Asia, the Gulf, the UK, Europe, Australia and North America. Overlap hours, shared dashboards and one accountable point of contact — wherever you are.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Remotie, Dhaka, Bangladesh

Remotie (“Remotie”, “we”, “us”) is a growth marketing agency based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, serving clients internationally. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our services, why we collect it, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit the contact form we collect your name, email address, phone number, location and the details of your enquiry. When you book a meeting we collect the name, email and any details you enter into the Google Calendar booking page. If you contact us by WhatsApp, email or LinkedIn, we hold that correspondence.

Information collected automatically. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, including IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and timestamps, for security and reliability. This site sets no advertising cookies of its own.

Client account data. Where you engage us as a client, we may be granted delegated access to your advertising, analytics, social and CRM accounts. We process the data inside those systems solely to deliver the services you have asked for, under your instructions.

2. How and why we use it
  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare for a scheduled call.
  • To provide, manage and improve the services set out in our agreement with you.
  • To send operational messages such as reports, invoices and service updates.
  • To keep records required for accounting, tax and contractual purposes.
  • To protect the site and our systems against fraud, abuse and security incidents.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use enquiry details to build advertising audiences, and we do not add you to a marketing list without your consent. Where consent is our basis for processing, you may withdraw it at any time.

3. Legal bases for processing

Where the laws of the EU, the UK or a comparable jurisdiction apply to you, we rely on: contract (to deliver services you have requested or take pre-contract steps), legitimate interests (to run, secure and improve our business in a way that does not override your rights), consent (for optional marketing communications), and legal obligation (for tax and record-keeping duties). For visitors in Bangladesh, we process personal data in accordance with applicable Bangladeshi law and the contractual terms agreed with you.

4. Service providers we rely on

To operate this website and our business we use a small number of trusted processors. Each holds your data only to perform its function:

  • Formspree — receives and forwards contact form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google Workspace & Google Calendar — email correspondence and meeting scheduling.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — messaging, where you choose to contact us on that channel.
  • LinkedIn and Instagram — social profiles we link to; their own policies govern any interaction there.
  • Our website host — serving this site and maintaining security logs.

We may also disclose information where required by law, court order or a valid request from a competent authority, or to establish and defend legal claims.

5. International transfers

Remotie operates from Bangladesh and works with clients and providers in other countries, so your information may be transferred to and stored outside your country of residence, including in the United States and the European Union. Where such a transfer is subject to data protection law, we take reasonable steps — including contractual safeguards with our processors — to protect your information to a comparable standard.

6. Retention

Enquiries that do not become engagements are kept for up to 24 months so we can pick up the conversation if you return, then deleted. Client records, contracts and financial documents are retained for the duration of the engagement and for the period required by applicable tax and accounting rules. Delegated access to your advertising and analytics accounts is revoked on request or at the end of the engagement.

7. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies by default. It uses your browser’s local storage only to remember interface preferences, and Google Fonts is loaded to render typography. If we later add analytics or advertising tags, this section and a cookie notice will be updated before those tags go live.

8. Your rights and choices

Subject to the law that applies to you, you may request access to the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict certain processing, request a portable copy, or withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email info.remotie@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may contact the relevant supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.

9. Security

We use access controls, multi-factor authentication on business accounts, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and least-privilege access to client systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data and the law requires it, we will notify you and the appropriate authority without undue delay.

10. Children and changes to this policy

Our services are directed at businesses and are not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

We may update this policy as our services change. The revision date at the top of this section always reflects the current version, and material changes will be highlighted on this page.
